Monitoring Malaysia's Plant Biodiversity Together

Help us explore identify record protect Malaysia's plants

Pl@netMatrix brings scientists and everyday nature lovers together to record, identify and protect the plants that make Malaysia one of the richest places on Earth.

Start exploring Identify a plant

Banner photo: Pinterest

Did You Know?

Malaysia is one of about seventeen megadiverse countries in the world. Its forests hold thousands of flowering plant species, and many of them are found nowhere else.

About the Project

Malaysia is a global biodiversity hotspot, but its plants are also under serious pressure. A growing population and the need for more farmland mean that forests are shrinking, and many species are becoming harder to find. To protect these plants, scientists first need to know where they grow and how their numbers are changing.

That is where you come in. Citizen science is a partnership between professional scientists and members of the public. Every photo you share adds another data point, helping researchers track Endangered, Rare and Threatened (ERT) plants across the country much faster than they could on their own.
