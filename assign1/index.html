Monitoring Malaysia's Plant Biodiversity Together

Help us explore identify record protect Malaysia's plants

Pl@netMatrix brings scientists and everyday nature lovers together. Join us in recording, identifying and protecting the plants that make Malaysia one of the richest places on Earth.

Start exploring Identify a plant

Banner photo: Pinterest

Did You Know?

Malaysia is one of only about seventeen megadiverse countries in the world. Its forests are home to thousands of flowering plants, and many of them grow nowhere else.

About the Project

Malaysia is packed with plant life, yet much of it is under real pressure. As towns grow and more land is cleared for farming, the forests are shrinking, and many plants are getting harder to find. Before scientists can protect them, they need to know where these plants still grow and whether their numbers are falling.

That's where you come in. Citizen science simply means scientists and ordinary people working side by side. Each photo you share is one more clue, and together those clues help researchers keep track of Endangered, Rare and Threatened (ERT) plants across the country far faster than they could alone.
