Why Every Photo Counts

Every verified photo is a record of where a plant was growing. Put together, these records help scientists map where Endangered, Rare and Threatened plants still survive and where they are disappearing.

How a Photo Reaches This Gallery

  1. A plant spotter photographs a plant in the wild.
  2. They upload the photo to iNaturalist or Pl@ntNet.
  3. The community checks the photo and confirms the plant's name.
  4. We choose the clearest photos for Pl@netMatrix.
  5. The photo appears here as a verified card.

Where Our Photos Come From

iNaturalist
A worldwide community where people share photos of plants and animals, and other members help identify them.
Pl@ntNet
A plant identification project where people upload plant photos and scientists use them to study plant life.
