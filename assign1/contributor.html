How to Become a Contributor

  1. Register as a Pl@netMatrix user.
  2. Take clear photos of the leaves, flowers, fruit or bark.
  3. Fill in the contribution form with the plant's name and your comments.
  4. Wait for a taxonomist to verify your upload.
  5. See your photo appear on this page.

What the Labels Mean

Verified
A taxonomist has checked the photo and confirmed the plant's name.
Pending review
The photo has been received and is waiting to be checked.
