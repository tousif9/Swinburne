Demographic information

  • Studying: BA - ICT, Swinburne University of Technology Sarawak
My hometown Dhaka is intense, in the best way. It's one of the most crowded cities in the world, a place where centuries of Mughal history meet fast-moving modern life along the Buriganga River. It's known for its mosques and its rickshaws, and Bangladesh is famous for its fine muslin and jamdani textiles. The street food is legendary too: jhalmuri and fuska are the snacks I grew up with.
A great achievement I was chosen as one of only 30 participants for Project Shohojatri, a community service programme run by BRAC University. Working with the team on grassroots projects taught me more about leadership and teamwork than any classroom could.
Favourite sports Football (playing and watching), swimming and badminton. Football is the one I never get tired of, whether I'm on the pitch or on the couch.
Favourite movies and series I watch a lot of films and series, but Interstellar is probably my favourite film of all time.
Favourite music Classic rock and alternative, especially Bryan Adams and Green Day. I also listen to Metallica, Pink Floyd and The Weeknd, so my playlist runs all the way from heavy metal and psychedelic rock to modern pop and R&B.
Favourite food Nothing beats a plate of Bangladeshi biryani: fragrant spiced rice cooked with meat. For a quick snack, I'll always reach for jhalmuri (spicy puffed rice tossed with onion, chilli and mustard oil) or fuska (crispy hollow shells filled with spiced potato and tangy tamarind water).
