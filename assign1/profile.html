My hometown Dhaka is intense. It is one of the most densely populated cities in the world, where centuries of Mughal history meet rapid modernisation, all centred around the Buriganga River. The city is known for its mosques and rickshaws, and Bangladesh is famous for its muslin and jamdani textiles and its biryani (fragrant spiced rice cooked with meat). The street food is legendary: jhalmuri (spicy puffed rice tossed with onion, chilli and mustard oil) and fuska (crispy hollow shells filled with spiced potato and tangy tamarind water) are the casual snacks I grew up with.
A great achievement I was selected as one of only 30 participants for Project Shohojatri, a community service programme run by BRAC University. Working with the team on grassroots initiatives was intense, and it taught me more about leadership and collaboration than any classroom could.
Favourite sports Football (both playing and watching), swimming and badminton.
Favourite movies and series I watch a lot of films and series, but Interstellar is probably my favourite film ever.
Favourite music Classic rock and alternative, especially Bryan Adams and Green Day.
Favourite food Biryani, jhalmuri and fuska. I'm always hunting for good street food or a plate of biryani, wherever I am.
