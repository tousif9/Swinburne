Identify a Plant

Tips for a Good Photo

  • Photograph one leaf, flower or fruit at a time, and keep it in sharp focus.
  • Use natural daylight and try to avoid strong shadows.
  • If you can, take both sides of a leaf. The underside often shows the veins more clearly.
  • Please never pick or damage a plant that might be endangered.

How Identification Works

  1. You upload a photo of a leaf, flower or fruit.
  2. The tool looks at features such as leaf shape, vein pattern and colour.
  3. It compares them with checked photos in our records.
  4. You see the closest matches, each with a score showing how sure the tool is.
  5. Plant experts look over any uncertain results to keep our records accurate.
