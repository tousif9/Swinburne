Identify a Plant

Tips for a Good Photo

  • Photograph a single leaf, flower or fruit in sharp focus.
  • Use natural daylight and avoid strong shadows.
  • Include both sides of a leaf if you can; the underside often shows the veins clearly.
  • Never pick or damage a plant that could be endangered.

Upload a Photo

How Identification Works

  1. You upload a photo of a leaf, flower or fruit.
  2. The engine measures features such as leaf shape, vein pattern and colour.
  3. These features are compared with verified photos in our database.
  4. The closest matches are shown with a confidence score.
  5. Taxonomists review uncertain results to keep the database accurate.

Best match: Dipterocarpus alatus

Scientific name
Dipterocarpus alatus (family Dipterocarpaceae)
Common names
Yang na, hairy-leaf apitong
Main location
Lowland forests of mainland Southeast Asia, often near rivers, from Myanmar and Thailand to Cambodia, Laos and Vietnam.
Conservation status
Listed as threatened because of logging and forest clearing.
Care tips and the right environment
  • Light: young plants like partial shade; older trees need full sun.
  • Soil: deep, moist and well-drained soil.
  • Climate: warm tropical lowlands with a wet season.
  • Space: grows into a very tall canopy tree, so it needs plenty of room.
