Our Contributors

Why Every Photo Counts

All pictures that are checked are records of the location of a plant growing. Combined, these records aid scientists in tracking the locations of Endangered, Rare and Threatened plants' holdouts and extirpations.

How a Photo Reaches This Gallery

  1. A plant spotter snaps a photo of a plant in the wild.
  2. They upload it to iNaturalist or Pl@ntNet.
  3. Other members verify the name of the plant by looking at the photo.
  4. For Pl@netMatrix we select the clearest photographs.
  5. The photo is shown here as a checked card.

Where Our Photos Come From

iNaturalist
A global community where people post photos of plants and animals and others help identify them.
Pl@ntNet
A plant identification project in which citizens upload plant photos and scientists analyze them to learn about plants.
