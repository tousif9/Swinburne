Identify a Plant

Tips for a Good Photo

  • Take photos of one leaf, flower or fruit at a time, and keep it in sharp focus.
  • Make use of natural light and avoid extreme lighting effects.
  • If you can, take both sides of a leaf. Often the veins are more prominent on the underside.
  • Please never pick or damage a plant that might be endangered.

How Identification Works

  1. You upload a picture of a leaf, flower or fruit.
  2. The tool considers characteristics like leaf shape, vein pattern and colour.
  3. It checks them against our records of checked photos.
  4. You will see the closest matches, with a score indicating how certain the tool is.
  5. Any questionable results are reviewed by plant experts to ensure accurate records.
